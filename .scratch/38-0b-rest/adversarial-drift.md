**Noise band: not measured this run** (the `--noise-repeat` control did not run); differences smaller than ~10-20% (the band slices 12/29 measured on this shared box) must be treated as noise-ambiguous.

**Ladder under-power guard** (per workload): `adversarial` UNDER-POWERED - T1 NOT-MEASURABLE; `drift` bracketed (at least one arm failed the top step).

When every arm sustains the ladder's top step the "highest sustained load level" metric ties by construction, so T1 is reported NOT-MEASURABLE rather than as a tie.
