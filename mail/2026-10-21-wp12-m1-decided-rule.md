# WP-12 — the "3 of 5" is rule-invariant, and the reason is that no M1 record ever had a service ceiling

**Node whose roadmap this changes:** `flashdb` — `docs/plan/open-items.md` leverage item 2 (and row
`T-84`) and the external headline at `README.md:73`; the reasoning line, not the number.

**The finding, in the one sentence the package exists for.** At 200 rps `flashdb-declared` sustains
**3 of 5** paired blocks under the decided lag-only rule (`sustained_primary`) against **sqlite 5/5**
and **postgres 5/5** — byte-for-byte the both-ceilings number from the old reducer — so the headline
does **not** change. It cannot change: no saved M1 arm-run was ever scored with a service ceiling
(`summarize_curve(windows, None)` at `harness/flashdb_harness/baseline_0b.py:1228` and `:1869`;
`"service_ceiling_us": null` in **45 of 45** window files; `service_ceiling_reported_windows = 0` in
every stored `curve[0]`). **0 of 45 rows differ.** So `T-84`'s premise in the dispatch ("the README's
3 of 5 is still the both-ceilings number") is right about the digits and wrong about the rule: those
verdicts were already lag-only, and the "both ceilings" label on ticket 82 never had a second ceiling
in it.

**What that leaves the README line meaning.** The 3-of-5 is lost inside one ~575 ms group per block,
not on service latency: the only rejections in the whole run are two single windows — b2 and b3, window
10 of 20, lag p99 **30,430 us** and **33,747 us** against 10,000 us, sitting on `group_elapsed_us`
**573,458 us** and **583,421 us**. flashdb's service p99 (403 ms median at 200 rps) is a real defect on
KPI 3 and it rejects nothing here. Suggested wording, if a line changes at all: "3 of 5 blocks at 200
rps, lost to two single-window lag breaches behind one ~575 ms group; service latency is the worst of
the three arms and is reported, not used as the throughput rejection."

**Sensitivity, so nobody reads "0 differ" as "the service ceiling is harmless".** Re-scored with a
10 ms service ceiling switched on (`summarize_curve(windows, 10_000)` — the counterfactual, not the
decided rule): **38 of 45 rows flip** and at 200 rps **all three arms read 0 of 5** (sqlite/postgres
would read 1/5 at 50 and 100 rps too). The ceiling cannot rank arms because the machine's own fsync p99
is 9,821 us and 40 of 45 rung-level service p99 values sit above 10,000 us. That is
`PLAN-ceiling-reconciliation`'s defect demonstrated on ticket 82's own records — it belongs to the
win/adversarial 96 rows, and it does not touch the external headline.
